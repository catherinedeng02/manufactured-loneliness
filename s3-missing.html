Section Three

Something Is
Still Missing.

When a companion brand grows lonely, who does it turn to?

Sections One and Two end at the same place from opposite directions. The factory can make anything; the world will buy it. Neither can produce the one thing the business actually runs on — a character people decide to care about. Pop Mart has never made a large acquisition. This section asks what it would buy if it had to, what the comparable companies did when they reached this point, and whether the missing asset is the kind of thing that can be bought at all.
